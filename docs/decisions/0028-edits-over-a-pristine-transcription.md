# 0028: Corrections are edits over a pristine transcription

Status: inherited · Date: 2026-11-18

## Decision

The transcription is kept untouched, and every correction is an entry in a list of edits applied over it by `applyEdits` (`src/core/edits`), the same way picture retouches are a list over the original pixels. Edits are keyed to assembled blocks by derived ids (`p{page}b{index}`) and carry the block's whole text with emphasis written as `<i>` and `<b>` tags, exactly as `drive.mjs body` hands it back. `corrections.md` is generated by comparing the pristine text with the edited one (`drive.mjs corrections`).

## Why (plain English)

The reading is the one expensive thing. Keeping it untouched gives undo, lets the book be rebuilt with every correction still on it, lets lost markup be repaired from the original, and makes a sheet of corrections possible at all.

## What we gave up

Editing text directly, which is simpler. Edits must be written against the assembled block, not a raw page: assembly joins paragraphs across page seams, so a page's text is not a block's text.

## Rule for agents

Write corrections against `drive.mjs body` output, with the tags it hands back; never against a raw leaf or a home-made reconstruction. Never edit the pristine transcription to make a correction. Do not replace without a new decision record.
